---
title: "Critique"
description: "Vision-language analysis of a sprite or animation."
---

Analyzes a sprite or animation and surfaces issues: pose continuity errors, palette violations, missing frames, pivot drift, style inconsistency.

Results appear in the Critique panel as a clickable list. Click any finding to jump to the relevant frame. Invoke via `AI > Critique`.

**Backend:** Vision-language model (Claude or GPT class). Palette violation check is objective and reliable; pose and style checks vary by model.
