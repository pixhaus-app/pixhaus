Generates a complete Wang edge-blob (47-tile) autotile set from 1-3 example transition drawings. Draw the base tile and one or two examples; the verb fills in the remaining positions.

Invoke via `AI > Tile`. Map your drawn tiles to blob positions in the preview grid, then run.

**Backend:** Image generation with strong style conditioning.
