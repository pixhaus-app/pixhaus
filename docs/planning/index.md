## How to read this archive

If you want the deep dive on one tool, open its file. If you want patterns across tools, open the synthesis folder. The category READMEs sit one layer above and explain how the tools in that category relate to each other.

Pricing was current as of May 2026. Subscription tiers change; verify before quoting.
