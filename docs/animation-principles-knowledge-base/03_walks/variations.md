# Walk Variations — A Catalog of Characters by Their Gait

*"All walks are different. No two people in the world walk the same."*

This is a catalog of distinct walks Use these as prompt presets.

## How to read this catalog

For each walk, the variables tracked are:
- **Tempo** — frames per step at 24fps
- **Up/down on head** — how much the head bobs
- **Lean** — body angle from vertical
- **Step length** — short / medium / long
- **Arm swing** — small / medium / wide
- **Special** — any non-default behaviors

## The Sneak Walk (Tradicional)

**Tempo:** 24-32 frames per step (very slow)
**Up/down:** Minimal — held low throughout
**Lean:** Forward as foot reaches; body weight transfers slowly
**Step length:** Long forward reach but slow
**Arm swing:** Arms held out for balance, NOT opposing legs
**Special:**
- Held PASSING pose is critical — character pauses with one leg lifted
- Body weight stays back as foot reaches forward
- Knee lifts high before each step
- Head leans back slightly during reach

**Prompt:**
> "Character sneaks forward with exaggerated caution. Each step takes about 1.5 seconds. The reaching foot extends fully forward while the body weight stays back. The character holds the passing pose briefly with knee high in the air. Arms held out at sides for balance, not swinging in opposition. Body bent forward slightly. No bouncing of head."

## The Tip-Toe Sneak (A Hurtadillas de Puntillas)

**Tempo:** 10-14 frames per step
**Up/down:** Small (head stays mostly level)
**Lean:** Slight forward lean
**Step length:** Short
**Arm swing:** Small, often held close to body
**Special:**
- Toes touch the ground first (not heel)
- Character looks self-aware, almost playful
- Faster than tradicional sneak — more confident
- Can be on threes or fours for extremely fast sneaks

**Prompt:**
> "Character tip-toes forward on the balls of feet. Quick light steps, about 12 frames per step. Body is poised and ready. Arms held close. Head stays nearly level — no bobbing. Quick but quiet, confident in sneaking."

## The Backward Sneak (A Hurtadillas Hacia Atrás)

**Tempo:** 24+ frames per step
**Special:** Same as forward sneak but body leans BACK and feet reach behind. Used to convey fear or retreat.

**Prompt:**
> "Character backs away cautiously. Each foot reaches backward while body weight stays forward (the opposite of normal). Slow held passing poses. Body trembling slightly with fear, head turning to check behind."

## The Strut / Swagger (Pavoneo)

**Tempo:** 12-16 frames per step
**Up/down:** Large
**Lean:** Slight forward, chest puffed
**Step length:** Long
**Arm swing:** Wide
**Special:**
- Shoulders roll noticeably
- Hips swing dramatically
- Toes turn out, almost duck-walk
- Chest leads the body
- Head bobs significantly with each step

**Prompt:**
> "Character struts with confidence. Each step is long and deliberate, 12-14 frames per step. Shoulders roll opposite to hips. Chest puffed forward, head held high but bobs slightly with each step. Arms swing wide. Toes turn slightly outward. Belt line tilts dramatically with each step."

## The Heavy Walk

**Tempo:** 16-24 frames per step
**Up/down:** Large
**Lean:** Forward
**Step length:** Medium
**Arm swing:** Small (arms hang heavy)
**Special:**
- Deep DOWN pose — knee bent significantly
- Long held DOWN — character "rests" on each step
- Head bobs deeply
- Shoulders drop with each step
- Dust puffs on each footfall (for very heavy)

**Prompt:**
> "Heavy character walks slowly with effort. Each step about 18 frames. Body sinks deeply on each footfall — visible knee bend. Head bobs heavily with each step. Arms hang down with minimal swing. Body leans forward. Brief held pause at the lowest point of each step. Visible weight in every motion."

## The Light / Bouncy Walk

**Tempo:** 8-10 frames per step
**Up/down:** Large (but quick)
**Lean:** Slight forward
**Step length:** Medium-short
**Arm swing:** Wide and quick
**Special:**
- Feet barely touch the ground
- Up pose almost airborne
- Energetic, optimistic
- Head bouncing with energy

**Prompt:**
> "Light bouncy walk, almost skipping. About 8 frames per step. Body lifts high on the UP pose. Quick light footfalls. Arms swing energetically. Head bobs rapidly with high energy. Character feels light and happy."

## The Dejected Walk

**Tempo:** 24-32 frames per step
**Up/down:** Minimal (dragging)
**Lean:** Forward, shoulders slumped
**Step length:** Short, shuffling
**Arm swing:** Almost none, arms hang dead
**Special:**
- Head hangs low
- Eyes downcast
- Feet drag slightly
- No spring in step
- Slight side-to-side drift (no purpose)

**Prompt:**
> "Defeated, dejected walk. Slow, about 28 frames per step. Shoulders slumped, head hung low. Feet shuffle, barely lifting. Arms hang lifelessly. No bounce in the body. Slight drift, no clear direction. Body language of total defeat."

## The Drunk Walk

**Tempo:** Erratic — varies wildly per step
**Up/down:** Erratic
**Lean:** Changes direction unpredictably
**Step length:** Inconsistent
**Arm swing:** Independent of legs, often flailing for balance
**Special:**
- Head is "stationary" (drunk fights to keep head still as if to convince observers he's not drunk)
- Each body part moves independently
- Sudden over-corrections
- Wide stance
- Occasionally near-falling

**Prompt:**
> "Drunk character walks erratically. Step timing varies — some quick, some painfully slow. Body weight constantly off-balance, with sudden corrections. Head held deliberately still while body lurches around it. Arms move independently of legs, flailing for balance. Occasional brief stops mid-step. Wide stance. Each body part seems to move on its own timing."

## The Angry / Stomping Walk

**Tempo:** 10-14 frames per step
**Up/down:** Heavy, emphatic
**Lean:** Forward, aggressive
**Step length:** Long, planted
**Arm swing:** Tense, fists clenched
**Special:**
- Foot accent DOWN with force
- Brief held impact on each footfall
- Shoulders tight and high
- Head leads aggressively

**Prompt:**
> "Angry stomping walk. About 12 frames per step but with heavy emphasis on each footfall. Each foot SLAMS down with force — brief impact frame held. Shoulders tense and raised. Fists clenched. Head leans forward aggressively. Body language explosive."

## The Cute / Mincing Walk

**Tempo:** 6-10 frames per step
**Up/down:** Small
**Lean:** Slightly back, dainty
**Step length:** Very short
**Arm swing:** Small, hands often held in front
**Special:**
- Toes turned in (knock-knee style)
- Quick light steps
- Hands often held up in front (like little paws)
- Body slightly bent

**Prompt:**
> "Cute mincing walk. Quick short steps, 8 frames each. Toes turned slightly inward. Body slightly bent forward. Hands held up in front, near chest. Light hops between steps. Quick small head bobs. Character feels small and demure."

## The Sexy / Sashay Walk

**Tempo:** 12-16 frames per step
**Up/down:** Minimal (head stays level)
**Lean:** Slight back
**Step length:** Medium, with feet crossing centerline
**Arm swing:** Small, with shoulder rolls
**Special:**
- Feet cross over the body's centerline
- Hips swing dramatically side-to-side
- Pelvis pushes forward slightly
- Body rotates around the hips like a duck
- Eyes maintain sustained eye contact
- Almost no head bounce — feline gliding

**Prompt:**
> "Sexy sashay walk. About 14 frames per step. Feet cross over the centerline, making the hips swing dramatically side-to-side. Pelvis pushes slightly forward. Body weight rotates around the hips. Head stays nearly level — no bounce. Small smile, sustained eye contact. Arms swing minimally but shoulders roll."

## The Sailor Walk

**Tempo:** 12-14 frames per step
**Up/down:** Medium
**Lean:** Side-to-side (rocking)
**Step length:** Wide stance
**Arm swing:** Medium, with body roll
**Special:**
- Whole body rocks side-to-side as if compensating for ship motion
- Wide stance for balance
- Head delays behind body roll
- Bowlegged appearance

**Prompt:**
> "Sailor walk. Wide stance, body rocks dramatically side-to-side with each step. About 14 frames per step. Head delays behind body roll. Arms swing with body weight transfer. Bowlegged appearance. Feels like someone used to ship motion."

## The Baby / Toddler Walk

**Tempo:** 8-12 frames per step
**Up/down:** Large (constant correction)
**Lean:** Constantly correcting
**Step length:** Short, wide stance
**Arm swing:** Arms held out wide for balance
**Special:**
- Constantly off-balance
- Wide stance for stability
- Arms held out for balance, not opposing legs
- Knee lifts very high (exaggerated)
- Each step is more of a controlled fall

**Prompt:**
> "Toddler walking. Wide stance, arms held out for balance. Each step is a near-fall caught at the last moment. Knees lift very high (almost exaggerated). About 10 frames per step but very wobbly. Body constantly correcting balance."

## The Old / Cane Walk

**Tempo:** 20-28 frames per step
**Up/down:** Small (no energy for big bobs)
**Lean:** Forward, hunched
**Step length:** Short, shuffling
**Arm swing:** Cane provides third support point
**Special:**
- Cane plants ahead of foot
- Three-point gait
- Hunched back
- Shaky legs
- Slow held passing pose

**Prompt:**
> "Elderly character walking with cane. Slow, 24 frames per step. Hunched back, body leans forward over the cane. Cane plants first, then the foot follows. Short shuffling steps. Almost no bounce in body. Hands tremble slightly. Head held forward."

## The Stair Walk (Going Up)

**Special:**
- Foot lifts higher to clear the step
- Foot plants FLAT on the step (not toe-first)
- Back leg stays bent
- Body leans forward into the climb
- Slower than ground walk

## The Stair Walk (Going Down)

**Special:**
- Body squats more than normal step
- Forward foot reaches down and lands toe-first
- Knee bends deeply on landing
- Slower than ground walk, more cautious

## The Military March

**Tempo:** 8-12 frames per step (precisely timed)
**Up/down:** Minimal (suppressed)
**Lean:** Upright
**Step length:** Standardized, identical each step
**Arm swing:** Precise, mechanical
**Special:**
- Counter-reaction suppressed — body moves as one block
- Cadence is RIGID (no variation between steps)
- Arms swing in precise arcs
- Heel strikes first, deliberately
- Each step identical to the next

**Prompt:**
> "Military march. Precise 10 frames per step, identical every step. No body counter-reaction — torso, head, and limbs move as a single coordinated unit. Arms swing in precise mechanical arcs. Heel strikes first. No natural body sway. Disciplined, robotic timing."

## Building your own walk

**understand the rules, then break them.**

A walk is defined by:
1. **Tempo** (frames per step)
2. **Up/down amount** (head bob)
3. **Lean direction** (forward/back/side)
4. **Step length** (short/long)
5. **Arm swing pattern** (normal opposition / sneak balance / wide / minimal)
6. **Foot action** (heel-first / flat / toe-first)
7. **Hip behavior** (normal / wide swing / sliding)
8. **Special tics** (cane, limp, drag, hop, etc.)

Mix and match these to create any character. A "fast-tempo, heavy up/down, forward-lean, long-step, wide-arm-swing, heel-first, dramatic-hip" walk is Mr. Macho. Change any one variable and you get a different character.
